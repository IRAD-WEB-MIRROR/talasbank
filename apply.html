Apply for a card online
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

Send an application for a Talasbank Classic or Talasbank Dollar card and choose the branch where you will collect it. You get an application number at once; with it you can follow the application on this page. The branch registers the application on the next working day. If the branch needs your signature on the application (form TB-K1), it will ask you to come with your passport within 10 working days; otherwise you sign when you collect the card.

Please noteDo not enter your name, passport or card numbers on this page. Talasbank never asks for your PIN or for the code from an SMS. Cards are kept at the branch for 60 days and then destroyed. Salary cards are issued only through your employer's salary agreement with the bank.

What happens next

  • The bank's Security Service checks the application against the information the bank holds.
  • Cards are made at the Card Personalisation Centre at the head office in Talas and embossed on Wednesdays. The card and the PIN envelope go to the branch with the cash collection service.
  • A US dollar current account is opened for a Talasbank Dollar card.
  • The bank may decline to issue a card without giving reasons.
