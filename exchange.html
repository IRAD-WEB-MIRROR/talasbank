Currency exchange
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

The exchange offices of Talasbank buy US dollars, Russian roubles and Kazakh tenge in cash, and sell US dollars, at the official rate of the Bank of Irad on the day of the operation. Work out the amount below and take a number for the exchange cash desk of the office you will visit. Bring the banknotes and your passport.

Opening hours of the exchange cash desksThe exchange cash desk opens with the branch and stops taking operations 30 minutes before the branch closes. On weekdays it is closed for lunch from 13:00 to 14:00 and for cash collection from 11:00 to 11:15. The office in the air terminal arrivals hall has no breaks. The hours of the office you choose are shown under its name. On public holidays the exchange cash desks in branches are closed.

Rules for cash operations

  • Banknotes are accepted whole and clean: no tears, writing, stamps or tape. US dollar notes of series before 1996 and damaged notes are accepted only for collection, not for exchange.
  • Coins are not accepted. Roubles are accepted in multiples of 50, tenge in multiples of 100. Som are paid out to the nearest 10 tyiyn below.
  • Sale of cash US dollars: up to US$300 per customer per calendar month at all banks of the Zone (Bank of Irad notice 2026/2), and only when cash dollars are available in the office. The cashier records each sale against your passport.
  • The rate is the official rate of the Bank of Irad on the day of the operation. A calculation made on another day is for information only.
  • Talasbank does not buy or sell currency at any other rate. Currency exchange outside licensed banks is illegal (Bank of Irad notice of 9 October 2026).
