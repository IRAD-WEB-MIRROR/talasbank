Exchange rates
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

Talasbank carries out currency operations at the official rates of the Bank of Irad. The rate of the US dollar is fixed at 8.40 som by the Bank of Irad Act 2008.

Official rates of the Bank of Irad

Cash operations in US dollarsUnder Bank of Irad notice 2026/2, cash withdrawals in US dollars and conversions of som into US dollars are limited to US$300 per customer per month at all banks of the Zone. Payments under state contracts are not limited. Sale of cash dollars is made when cash is available in the branch.

Talasbank does not buy or sell currency at other rates. Currency exchange outside licensed banks is illegal (Bank of Irad notice of 9 October 2026).

Exchange offices: in all branches, at the Talas Civil Air Terminal and at Bazar-i Kalan.
