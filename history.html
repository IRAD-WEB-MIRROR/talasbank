History of the bank
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

Talasbank was chartered by the Banking and Commercial Currency Act 1993 and is the largest commercial bank of Irad. Its licence of the Bank of Irad is No. B-001.

6 September 1993Talasbank opens for business
Chartered under the Banking and Commercial Currency Act 1993, the bank opens at the former Talas offices of the savings and agricultural banks. Accounts are kept in som and in US dollars.
14 March 1994Escrow department opens
The new escrow department receives export earnings under the Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority. Royalties and export duty are paid out first; the balance is credited to the exporter.
22 November 1999First share auction
The first block of the bank's shares was sold at public auction in Talas.
15 May 2001Second share auction
Shares of the bank were sold at the second public auction. The territorial administration keeps a minority stake.
1 October 2003Talasbank debit cards
The first Talasbank debit cards are issued for salaries and pensions, on the national card network built with Attelkom. Cash machines are working at the head office and at four branches in Talas.
1 June 2008Partner of the Anara Toktarova Children's Fund
From today donations to the new Children's Fund are accepted at every Talasbank branch without commission.
16 February 2009Accounts for clan councils
Talasbank opens settlement accounts for clan councils, so that payments for seasonal livestock contracts reach the council's account directly. Cash offices open in the district towns this year.
1 November 2010Talasbank Online: trial begins
Internet banking opens for a trial group of customers of the head office. Statements and transfers between your own accounts are available; other branches will follow.
3 April 2012Payments and transfers by SMS
With Attelkom, Talasbank customers can now pay for mobile service and send money to another Talasbank card by text message. Register at any branch with your card and passport.
8 September 2014A new look for Talasbank
From today the bank's signs, cards and forms carry the redrawn mark: the Talas Alatau and the river, in navy and gold. The 1993 sign stays on older branches and cash machines until they are refitted. Cards with the old design remain valid until their expiry date.
17 February 2025Second branch in Iyık Janyl
The Lower Town branch opens at 09:00 on Monday 17 February, with a cash machine and a currency exchange desk.
18 August 2025Five new cash machines
New cash machines are working at the Talas bus station, at Bazar-i Kalan and at the Talas Civil Air Terminal.
22 September 2025Agreement on the e-Irad payment gateway
Talasbank and the Digital Services Directorate signed an agreement to accept payments for state services on pay.e-irad.org. Card payments will follow the upgrade of the bank's card processing.
24 April 2026Annual Report 2025
The Annual General Meeting approves the Annual Report 2025.
