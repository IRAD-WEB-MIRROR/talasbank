Private clients
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

Accounts, deposits, loans and payments for individuals. Bring your passport (or ID card) to any branch. Talasbank serves about 610,000 private clients.

Current accounts

Accounts in som and in US dollars, opened in one visit. Pensions and public wages paid by the Government of the ISEZ and the city administrations are paid into Talasbank accounts; the card is issued with the account.

Your account by e-Irad temporary slip

While the e-Irad ID card is not issued and Talasbank Online accepts new clients only at the branch, account holders can see the balance and the last operations of their som current account by the number of the temporary reference slip printed from e-Irad. Information only: transfers and payments are made at a branch or in Talasbank Mobile.

Current interest rates are shown on the board in every branch. Deposits in US dollars are repaid in US dollars; cash repayment in dollars is subject to the monthly limit in force (Bank of Irad notice 2026/2), the balance is paid by transfer or in som at the official rate.

Payments

Pay for gas (NurGaz), electricity (Iradenergo), water, telephone and internet (Attelkom, Torgoy) at any branch without queueing at the utility's office, in Talasbank Mobile, or by SMS. State duties, fines and the road-use charge can be paid with a payment slip from pay.e-irad.org at any branch.

Talasbank Mobile

Som and dollar accounts in one application: balances, transfers between your accounts, payments for utilities. For Android 4.4 and above. Connect it at your branch with your passport; you will receive a login by SMS.

Safe deposit boxes

At the head office and the Altyn, Pochtamt and Azatlık branches.
