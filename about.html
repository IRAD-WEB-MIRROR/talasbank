About the bank
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

Talasbank (Bank of Talas) Open Joint-Stock Company was chartered by the Banking and Commercial Currency Act 1993 and opened for business in September 1993, from the Talas offices of the former savings and agricultural banks. It is the largest commercial bank of Irad.

History

1993 The bank is chartered and opens in September; dollar banknotes are flown in to Talas.
1994 The escrow department opens for export receipts, under the new Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority.
1999, 2001 Shares are sold at auction; the state keeps a minority stake.
2000s Debit cards and the national card network, with Attelkom.
2009 Accounts for the clan councils.
2012 SMS payments and transfers, with Attelkom.
2025 Second branch in Iyık Janyl; agreement on the e-Irad payment gateway.
