About the Tariffs

Commissions, interest rates on deposits and loans and the bank's charges are set only by the Tariffs in force on the day of the operation. Interest rates on deposits are shown on the board in every branch. The bank informs clients of changes to the Tariffs on the information boards and in the internet bank not less than 10 calendar days before they take effect.

Without commission in any case: payments to NurGaz, Talas Electric Networks and the city water service; donations to the Anara Toktarova Children's Fund; blocking a lost card through the information service 1993.

Deposits

Money on deposit and current accounts with Talasbank is not insured by a deposit guarantee fund. The bank keeps the reserves and capital ratios required by the Bank of Irad (capital adequacy 18.4% on 31 December 2025).

Deposits in US dollars are repaid in US dollars. Cash repayment in dollars is subject to the monthly limit set by Bank of Irad notice 2026/2; the balance is paid by transfer or in som at the official rate.

Complaints

A complaint is accepted in writing at any branch or at the head office, or by e-mail to info@talasbank.net. The bank answers within 15 working days. A client who does not agree with the answer may write to the Banking Supervision and Licensing Department of the Bank of Irad.
