News
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.
  • 20 October 2026Heating season: pay for gas at any branch

    The heating season has begun. Gas bills of NurGaz can be paid by the 25th of each month at any Talasbank branch without commission, in Talasbank Mobile with the account code from the receipt, or at the NurGaz cash desks.

  • 6 October 2026Card payments on pay.e-irad.org: stage 3 of 4

    The upgrade of card processing to the 3-D Secure standard, begun in November 2025, continues. The card module of the payment gateway for state services (pay.e-irad.org) is at stage 3 of 4; completion is now planned into November. Until then, payment slips printed from the gateway can be paid in cash at any branch or Irad Pochtasy counter.

  • 25 September 2026On card transactions involving Tulparcoin

    In accordance with the statement of the Bank of Irad of 24 September 2026, Tulparcoin is not legal tender. Talasbank does not process card payments for the purchase of Tulparcoin or for top-ups of Tulpar Pay wallets. Payments already made are not affected.

  • 1 June 2026Eighteen years with the Anara Toktarova Children's Fund

    Since 2008 Talasbank has been a partner of the Anara Toktarova Children's Fund. Donations to the Fund's account are accepted at every branch without commission.

  • 5 May 2026On cash withdrawals in US dollars

    In accordance with Bank of Irad notice 2026/2 of 4 May 2026, from May cash withdrawals in US dollars and conversions of som into US dollars are limited to US$300 per customer per month. Payments under state contracts are exempt. Balances in US dollars remain in US dollars and can be transferred without limit.

  • 24 April 2026Annual General Meeting approves the Annual Report 2025

    The Annual General Meeting of shareholders approved the Annual Report for 2025. Net profit rose by 15% to 586.5 million som; total assets passed 27.8 billion som; capital adequacy 18.4%. Annual Report 2025 (PDF).
