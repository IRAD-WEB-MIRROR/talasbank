General conditions of account and card service for private clients
This section is being translated. The text is given in English.

Public offer of Talasbank (Bank of Talas) OJSC. Approved by the Management Board on 12.01.2026 (minutes No. 2). In force from 01.02.2026; replaces the conditions of 03.02.2020.

1. The offer and its acceptance

1.1. Talasbank (Bank of Talas) Open Joint-Stock Company, general banking licence of the Bank of Irad No. B-001 (the Bank), offers private persons (the Client) to open and keep current accounts, deposit accounts and payment cards on these General conditions.

1.2. The Client accepts the offer by signing the application for opening an account, a deposit or a card at a branch, or form TB-K1 for a card ordered on the Bank's website. From that day these conditions, the application and the Tariffs of Talasbank for private clients form the agreement between the Client and the Bank.

1.3. The Bank may change these conditions and the Tariffs. The new text is placed on the information board of every branch and on talasbank.net not less than 10 calendar days before it takes effect. A Client who does not agree may close the account without a closing commission before that day.

2. Opening an account

2.1. An account is opened on presentation of the Client's ID card or passport. A foreign citizen also presents the residence permit or the migration card.

2.2. Accounts are kept in som and in US dollars. Operations in other currencies are made by conversion at the official rate of the Bank of Irad on the day of the operation.

2.3. The Bank may refuse to open an account without giving reasons.

3. Cards

3.1. A card is issued to the holder of a current account. It is ready in 5 working days and is handed to the holder in person at the branch named in the application. A card not collected within 60 days is destroyed.

3.2. The card and the PIN are given separately. The holder does not write the PIN on the card or tell it to anyone. The Bank never asks for the PIN or for the code from an SMS.

3.3. Cards of the Bank are accepted in Irad. Payments on pay.e-irad.org and other payments on the internet are accepted after the card processing upgrade announced by the Bank.

3.4. A lost or stolen card is blocked by calling the information service 1993 (24 hours). The holder is responsible for operations made before the call.

4. Payments and cash

4.1. The Bank executes the Client's orders within the balance of the account and the limits in force. Orders to other banks received before 15:00 on a working day are executed the same day through the Clearing House of the Bank of Irad.

4.2. Cash withdrawals in US dollars and conversions of som into US dollars are limited as set by the notices of the Bank of Irad (at present US$300 per client per month, notice 2026/2).

4.3. Commissions are charged under the Tariffs in force on the day of the operation and are debited from the account without a further order of the Client.

5. Deposits

5.1. The term, currency and interest of a deposit are written in the deposit agreement signed at the branch. Interest is calculated on the actual balance and the actual number of days.

5.2. Money on deposit and current accounts with Talasbank is not insured by a deposit guarantee fund. The bank keeps the reserves and capital ratios required by the Bank of Irad (capital adequacy 18.4% on 31 December 2025).

6. Statements and information

6.1. The Client may receive a statement at any branch, in Talasbank Online or through the SMS service. The Client checks the statement and reports any error within 30 days.

6.2. The Bank keeps the Client's information confidential and gives it to third parties only in the cases provided by law, at the written request of a court, the prosecutor or the Bank of Irad, or with the Client's written consent.

7. Disputes

7.1. Complaints are answered within 15 working days. Disputes not settled by negotiation are heard by the courts of the ISEZ at the place of the Bank's head office.

8. Closing an account

8.1. The Client may close an account at any time by an application at the branch. The balance is paid in cash, within the limits in force, or transferred to the account the Client names.

8.2. The Bank may close an account with no operations and no balance for three years, after notice to the Client by post.
